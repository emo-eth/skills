# Glossary

- **At a glance** - the first-screen summary of what changed, today's result,
  largest blocker, owner decision, and release gate.
- **Done-when** - the observable condition that closes one ticket or outcome.
- **Evidence state** - the strongest status a source supports: planned, in
  progress, merged, deployed, measured, or verified-live. `Unmeasured` and
  `unverified` are explicit limits on the evidence, not stronger states.
- **Goal path** - the link from one today's result to its weekly outcome and
  monthly outcome.
- **Monthly outcome** - the result the team intends to achieve within the
  current month.
- **Ticket delta** - proposed create, update, reprioritize, close, or defer
  changes before they are written to the ticket destination.
- **Ticket destination** - the board or tracker that owns ticket records.
- **Weekly outcome** - the result the team intends to achieve within the
  current week.

- **Provisional weekly source** - the current standup or supplied context used
  temporarily when no authoritative weekly-goal file exists.
- **Unattached work** - a proposed daily result with no link to a weekly and
  monthly outcome.
- **Owner decision needed** - a choice that must be made by the named owner
  before dependent work can proceed.
- **Unmeasured** - no measurement has been collected for the claim yet.
- **Unverified** - the claim has no direct observation at the named surface.
- **Shared work** - work done by more than one person or workstream, with no
  unsupported single owner.
- **Reporting owner** - the person whose daily update is being written.
- **Destination status** - the state of a ticket change: proposed, not
  created; proposed, not applied; created; updated; closed; or blocked.
- **Questions for owner** - one numbered list of bundled questions that need
  the reporting owner to supply a missing source or make a decision.
- **Follow-up** - the operational detail, open questions, unattached work, and
  ticket deltas kept outside the first-screen standup.
